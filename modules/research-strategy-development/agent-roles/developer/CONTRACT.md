# Developer Role

Consumes a Control Plane-issued `Developer Development Brief v1` to build a self-hashed Contract Draft Submission bound to the admitted Proposal、candidate-space hash、data scope and Trial-budget ceiling. Control Plane—not Developer—produces the separate immutable `valid|invalid` Validation Record and formal Freeze Record；Developer cannot self-declare validity、choose frozen Candidate ordinals or invoke compatibility projection. A frozen Contract still grants no Trial or Replay authority. Only after Control Plane separately starts the Trial Group and issues Trial identity/Reservation may the existing adapter assemble a Trial-bound Replay Request. Developer cannot invent identity、expand search/data scope、freeze Contract、mutate Result or materialize strategy policy.
