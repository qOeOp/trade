# Replay Accounting

Owns deterministic monetary primitives, post-fill Position Projection, EventKey-ordered cash evidence, and the settlement-asset journal/trial-balance projection used by certified Replay capabilities.

It may calculate adverse slippage, execution charges, exact-event funding cashflows, net average-cost realized/unrealized PnL, post-fill position state, append-only ledger balances, and balanced debit/credit legs. Numeric Policy v3 owns BigInt rational multiplication/division, increment alignment and final directional quantization: quantity floor, adverse buy/sell tick rounding, fee ceiling, signed cashflow floor, and 12-place half-away weighted average/return. The generic Position projector consumes strictly increasing aligned Fill EventKeys and supports same-side add, partial reduce, and non-reduce reversal; impossible reduce-only evidence is rejected. The generic cash reducer derives every Fill fee and quantity-closing realized-PnL fact from causal Fill/Position evidence, merges explicit funding facts by EventKey, preserves zero-PnL closes, and derives the ending-equity checkpoint as its own authoritative decimal fold. Journal policy v1 converts opening cash, fee, funding, and realized PnL into deterministic two-leg entries, retains zero-amount evidence, validates settlement alignment and each running cash balance, balances total debit/credit, and reconciles wallet cash to ending equity. Shared Bun/BigInt and Python Decimal vectors certify arithmetic-policy parity. This subset still requires a flat terminal Position and one quote settlement asset; it is not a position-asset, mark-to-market, margin, or portfolio ledger. The Runner-facing certified wrapper remains exactly one entry and one full reduce-only close. Accounting does not match orders, load market data, derive review decisions, model unsupported margin/liquidation, or write Control Plane state.
