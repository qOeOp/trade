# Replay Accounting

M4-P28 slice 7 owns the read-only bounded-cycle projector over committed slice-6 Accounting children. It accepts only Control Plane-predeclared cycles whose Risk and Trial Balance are full-flat；successor opening cash equals the predecessor committed `ending_available_cash`. It wraps every child Ledger/Journal entry without changing the child hash, removes only cycle 2+ `portfolio_opening_equity` from the consolidated view, proves exactly one sequence opening、balanced final cash/NAV、zero collateral/unrealized/current risk, and accumulates historical admission risk only when exactly matched by released risk. It cannot execute a Lane、mint/append a cycle or decide review/lifecycle.

M4-P28 slice 5 owns the read-only owner-keyed composition over committed slice-4 Risk Evidence and its exact Lane Result/Artifact pairs. Accounting never rebuilds fills、terminal selection or monetary classification：it preserves each non-zero source Ledger cashflow and every non-opening source Journal leg, replaces all Lane-local opening balances with one Portfolio opening equity, then binds every posting to `lane_id + risk_record_hash + final owner`. This is required because Funding/PnL may alter the live isolated-collateral account；forcing those facts into wallet cash would be a second incompatible ledger. The consolidated Trial Balance must exactly equal Risk Evidence available/settled cash、live collateral、unrealized/NAV and frozen/current risk bridge. Slice 6 只由 Runner 为该投影提交 companion Artifact；Accounting 本身仍无 publication、cycle 或 execution authority。

M4-P27 owns the read-only two-fixed-partial accounting and bounded-cycle roll-forward. The Sequence projector accepts only Control Plane-predeclared full-flat children, preserves every child Ledger/Journal hash, removes only cycle 2+ local opening postings from the consolidated view, and proves one opening equity plus final cash/NAV. Accounting cannot choose a terminal、replay matching、reinterpret risk or authorize/append a cycle.

M4-P16 adds a separate read-only accounting successor over the committed M4-P15 Protective Terminal Artifact. P15 terminal record is the Lane accounting key；entry fee, each exact pre-terminal Funding event, winning-owner realized PnL, generic terminal trading fee, liquidation fee, collateral release and open-at-end unrealized valuation are projected into a new Ledger/Journal/Trial Balance. Preempted P6 terminal events and applied post-terminal Funding are explicit hash-bound exclusions. P7 evidence remains immutable；P16 is authoritative only for one initial Allocation cycle with no reentry and must exactly reconcile P15 cash, collateral, unrealized and NAV.

It may calculate adverse slippage, trade/liquidation charges, exact funding, average-cost PnL, post-Fill Position, cash, Equity v3, Journal v5, Trial Balance, and Margin v7 snapshots. `never_opened` accepts no Fill/Position/Funding/Margin facts, preserves cash, does not reserve collateral, and may value either a GTC data-boundary close/mark or IOC expiry open. Each Margin snapshot must bind the venue-risk snapshot whose initial/maintenance parameters it actually used; mismatch is rejected. Liquidation fee is a distinct ledger fact and expense account; it cannot be folded into trade fee or deducted twice. Accounting does not resolve policy epochs or decide whether a source event triggers liquidation. Dynamic collateral, deficit/insurance/ADL, partial liquidation, grid-interior execution, borrow, cross margin, and shared portfolio allocation remain unsupported.
