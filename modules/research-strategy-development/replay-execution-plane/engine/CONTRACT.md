# Replay Reference Engine

Owns the deterministic EventKey total order, normalized instrument-delisting/bar/funding SourceEvent merge, append-only order lifecycle, fill, and position orchestration kernel for the certified Simulator Policy v2 vertical slice. Signal-time submission remains an explicit command fact; when the eligible entry `bar_open` is consumed, the SourceEvent reducer synchronously invokes the entry-order lane to commit `activate -> fill -> stop/target submit+activate`. It then advances the in-position lane until stop/target/EOD or terminal failure and synchronously invokes the exit-order lane. Entry funding exclusion, same-open gap handling, consumed source prefix and OrderEvents therefore share one causal reducer boundary rather than separate post-processing. Generic status/halt streams, external commands, multi-order matching and portfolio reduction remain outside the certified subset. Monetary, post-fill Position, cash-ledger, journal, and trial-balance facts are delegated to `../accounting`; input admission and derived measures are delegated to `../data-adapter` and `../metrics`.

The engine consumes an immutable request plus its bound Dataset Manifest and delegates normalization/admission before the first event. It never loads dataset locations itself, selects Candidates, evaluates promotion, writes Control Plane state, or invents unsupported fill semantics.
