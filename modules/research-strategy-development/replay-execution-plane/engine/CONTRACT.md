# Replay Reference Engine

Owns the deterministic EventKey total order and source-bound order orchestration for Simulator v7. Same-time phases are delisting `00`, funding `10`, exact mark/risk/liquidation `15`, and OHLC market `20`. A venue-risk epoch effective at time `t` is resolved before every SourceEvent at `t`; post-entry, path, terminal Margin and liquidation fee use that snapshot and persist its id/hash. Exact breach cancels stop/target, then submits, activates, and fully fills one forced reduce-only market order at `15.3/15.4/15.5`; non-breach Mark never triggers strategy orders. The execution price is policy-modelled from trigger Mark plus adverse slippage. OHLCV breach emits failure evidence without forced execution. Generic status/halt, tick/L2 execution, partial liquidation, deficit/insurance/ADL, external commands, general multi-order matching, and portfolio reduction remain outside the certified subset.

The engine consumes an immutable request plus its bound Dataset Manifest and Decision Evidence Timeline v1, then rebuilds the sole Entry's Snapshot from admitted facts. `signal_time_complete` requires one attested Entry containing Bundle v1, Build Attestation v1 and Receipt v3；`mode=none` may deterministically synthesize only one precomputed-order compatibility Entry。Missing/tampered Timeline, wrong sequence/time/Order hash, invalid member evidence or direct required-lane bypass is rejected before market execution. Timeline v1 cardinality is exactly one；it does not authorize dynamic decisions or multiple Orders.

Engine Checkpoint v8 is a non-authoritative execution-state envelope emitted after all effects of one source event have completed. It binds Request v15/data/simulator/numeric policy, Decision Timeline hash及其派生 member hashes/policies、source prefix、entry/bracket state、funding/risk snapshots 与自哈希。Resume 必须复现 clean Result；Timeline hash 漂移即使 member 看似相同也拒绝。
