# Replay Execution Plane Contracts

Owns the versioned Replay request, Dataset Manifest, instrument-accounting spec, market input, fill, ledger, journal, trial-balance, result, artifact, and fingerprint contracts.

## Certified Result v29 / Simulator Policy v7 capability

- One asset and one isolated position lane.
- Closed-candle signal with earliest execution at the next bar open.
- Signal-time market submission remains inert until the eligible `bar_open` SourceEvent synchronously drives activation, entry fill, and bracket activation; same-time phase-`10` funding sees the `t-` flat position.
- Append-only submitted/activated/triggered/partial-or-full-filled/cancelled/rejected order events with a strictly increasing per-order sequence and nondecreasing event time.
- Every order event carries the total-order key `(event_time, boundary_phase, source_sequence, event_subphase, stable_event_id)`; equal or backward keys are rejected.
- Trial Reservation Snapshot v5 is owned and issued by Research Control Plane. It binds the half-open new-claim window `[issued_at, expires_at)` plus Request v20 risk/spec/decision schedules, supplemental-facts and supplemental-requirement-set hashes; expiry closes fresh/retry/resume-target admission but does not revoke an Attempt claimed inside the window. Runner validates identity, claim window, exact execution-spec hash, dataset/policy/harness hashes and the complete certified capability set before cancellation, idempotency lookup, engine execution or artifact publication. Replay does not query or mutate Trial state.
- Replay Attempt Lease v1, Checkpoint Receipt v2 and Resume Authorization Snapshot v1 are Control Plane-owned runtime authorities. Artifact v31 persists Decision Evidence Timeline v7; Run Outcome v26 carries current fencing and commit/failure state. Timeline Entry owns Decision Boundary v6, Market/State Snapshots and nullable Bundle/Build/Receipt. Result/Fingerprint/Checkpoint v13 bind Schedule, Boundary, Timeline, active protection and pending strategy-exit Order evidence. Attempt/checkpoint/receipt/authorization identity does not alter Result v29 economic fingerprint; decision and execution evidence does.
- Artifact Store Capability v1 freezes one Attempt-scoped port contract: immutable create, full-content SHA-256 collision identity, strong read-after-write, and Manifest-last publication. The certified local snapshot requires hard-link create-if-absent plus temporary unlink. The contract-valid remote target requires conditional put with `If-None-Match: *` and abort/expiry of uncommitted uploads, but it is not an executable capability until an adapter passes the same parity and crash certification.
- Dataset Manifest v7 carries non-empty, ordered, contiguous venue-risk and instrument-spec schedules with half-open validity. Request v20 and Result v29 fingerprint bind canonical schedule hashes; the instrument hash covers spec epochs plus the full-window accounting spec. The active epoch at `t` is selected before every SourceEvent at `t`; entry Request risk parameters must match the entry epoch, while each later Margin/liquidation calculation uses and records its event-time risk snapshot. Gap, overlap, incomplete coverage, unavailable epoch, symbol/venue drift or hash mismatch is a pre-event failure. Multiple spec provenance epochs are supported only while accounting fields remain invariant. This is frozen-input verification, not automatic historical venue reconstruction.
- Dataset Manifest v7 also declares one immutable supplemental revision stream and the canonical hash of `Supplemental Requirement Set v1`. Decision Schedule v4 and Timeline v7 bind every exact decision time/sequence/effect, closed-bar Market Snapshot, optional open-position State Snapshot, and input/build/runtime evidence. The certified sequence is pre-entry no-action, one initial entry, position-open no-action, at most one tighten-only full-position protective-stop replacement, and at most one final full-position reduce-only market exit. Replay cannot add times, change effects, loosen protection, alter the target, or widen either quantity.
- Normalized `instrument_delisted`, `funding`, `mark`, `bar_open`, and `bar_range` SourceEvents use the same key space; same-time ordering is policy epoch selection then phase `00 -> 10 -> 15 -> 20`, and Result v29 persists the consumed prefix only. Supplemental facts form a signal-time input view, not market execution SourceEvents. Mark is risk/valuation evidence; only an exact maintenance breach may create the system forced-liquidation lane.
- Conditional trigger evidence binds the OHLC source class and observed trigger price.
- Market entry and full reduce-only stop or take-profit exit, with sibling cancellation and fill-to-order traceability.
- One optional final strategy exit is opposite-side market, reduce-only, and full-open-position. It is submitted at the closed-bar decision and can activate/fill only at its frozen strictly later bar open. Exact risk, stop gap, and target gap win at the same open; terminal-before-decision becomes not-reached, while a submitted losing exit is cancelled.
- One optional protective-stop replacement is opposite-side stop-market, reduce-only, full-open-position, and tighten-only. At a non-terminal closed-bar decision, the old active stop is cancelled before the replacement is submitted and activated. The replacement must remain on the protective side of the target and must not already be crossed by the decision close; it cannot retroactively trigger inside that bar.
- Composition certification does not add a new wire capability: after replacement, the replacement stop is the sole stop authority. A later strategy-exit Fill cancels that stop and the target; an exact maintenance breach cancels the current stop, target, and any pending strategy exit before the forced Order; a stop Fill cancels the target and pending strategy exit. Every path permits exactly one terminal Fill, and checkpoint resume preserves the same owner/order transitions without re-evaluating committed position-open decisions.
- Direction-symmetry certification mirrors prices around a fixed center: long stop tightening upward maps to short stop tightening downward; mirrored entry/stop Fills produce equal signed PnL/return and identical OrderEvent phase/subphase sequences. If the post-replacement OHLC range touches both current stop and target, both sides select the stop-first conservative path and must publish `ohlcv-stop-target-collision` with `resolution_limited`. This is the certified simple-bracket envelope, not proof of the actual intrabar path or a generic multi-order OHLC resolver.
- A terminal market SourceEvent synchronously drives the certified exit-order lane; trigger/fill/sibling-cancel EventKeys remain causally bound to that source boundary.
- Conservative stop-first same-bar collision; stop/TP gap triggers at the observed bar open, then applies adverse slippage.
- Per-fill fee/slippage and exact timestamp funding events.
- Every Fill carries the exact filled OrderEvent key; fee/realized-PnL ledger facts reuse the causal Fill key, funding reuses its SourceEvent key, and initial/ending snapshots use explicit phase-`70`/`100` checkpoint keys.
- Every certified Fill produces a post-fill Position Projection with the same EventKey. Accounting separately certifies ordered multi-Fill net average-cost add/partial-reduce/reversal projection and a cash reducer that merges causal fee/realized-PnL/funding facts. The current Runner permits one non-reduce entry followed by a full reduce-only stop/target/strategy exit or an open EOD terminal Position; it does not expose partial strategy reduction or wider matching.
- Dataset Manifest v7 binds the full-window linear-derivative accounting spec, its PIT provenance schedule, supplemental declaration, and an explicit Mark capability. `mark_coverage=none` requires no Mark data; `complete_grid` requires exact event-time availability, strict timestamp/source-sequence order, full endpoint/grid/count coverage, tick alignment, and content-hash binding. Partial or lagged Mark streams are rejected rather than treated as exact.
- Result v29 binds Margin v7 and Journal v4. Request v20 freezes exact-risk full liquidation, forced-order priority, trigger-Mark adverse execution, full quantity, independent liquidation fee, and deficit rejection; event-time venue-risk epoch supplies maintenance parameters and liquidation fee. Exact breach embeds Liquidation Execution v2 plus Observation v3 with snapshot provenance and `execution_status=simulated_full_close`; OHLCV breach returns Run Outcome v26 failure evidence with `not_simulated`. Observation is never itself a Result or Fill.
- `rd-replay-number-v3` converts admitted finite number values into decimal coefficients, performs bps adjustment, notional fee, funding, linear PnL, weighted-average and return division as BigInt rationals, then quantizes once: quantity floors to step; buy execution price ceils and sell price floors to tick; fee expense ceils; signed funding/realized cashflow floors toward negative infinity; weighted average and return use half-away-from-zero at 12 decimal places. Stop/target, OHLC, initial cash, Fill, Position, Ledger and Journal facts must align. Shared language-neutral vectors are independently certified by Bun/BigInt and Python Decimal; this proves arithmetic-policy parity, not arbitrary JSON parser or unbounded-decimal transport equivalence.
- Deterministic Result Artifact and complete identity fingerprint, including numeric, journal, equity, and margin policy hashes/versions.
- Manifest-bound content hash, RFC 3339 UTC timestamps, explicit instrument lifecycle, universe survivorship, observed-through and bar/funding/mark availability policy.

The lifecycle component certifies deterministic partial quantity transitions and reduce-only caps; the market-data kernel does not claim real historical liquidity partials. At EOD, Simulator v7 cancels the current bracket and any pending strategy exit, creates no synthetic Fill, preserves the open Position, and uses the final exact Mark when certified, otherwise closed-bar close. Exact-risk liquidation and the authorized strategy exit are full-close only. The sole certified replacement is one full-position tighten-only protective stop; generic amend/cancel-replace, stop loosening, target replacement, continuous trailing, partial strategy reduction, grid-interior paths, limit queue, TIF, multi-entry/reversal, dynamic collateral, partial liquidation, deficit/insurance/ADL, cross/shared margin, and fast mode remain unsupported.

## Partial Reduce design seam (not certified)

`trade.rd-replay-partial-reduce-intent-draft.v1` is deliberately absent from Request v20, Decision Schedule v4 and `REPLAY_CERTIFIED_CAPABILITIES`. It freezes one opposite-side market reduce-only fixed quantity that is strictly smaller than the initial quantity, is decided only after entry eligibility, executes strictly after its closed-bar signal, and must leave the Position open. Draft protection policy `rd-replay-partial-reduce-protection-v1-draft` requires the partial Fill first, then—without an intervening SourceEvent—cancels both current protection orders and submits/activates replacements at the same trigger prices for exactly `abs(post-fill position)`. The first executable slice may allow one later final full exit but must reject stop replacement or a second partial reduce. Until non-terminal source reduction, partial Position Projection, ledger/checkpoint evidence and atomic bracket resize pass certification, any Reservation requiring `partial-reduce-v1-draft` is rejected before Engine execution and cannot publish Result or Artifact.

The certified Manifest only proves `delisted_at`, not halt/resume history or a settlement price. An open position reaching delisting therefore produces a non-retryable typed failure with the terminal EventKey and publishes no partial Result; the engine never invents a terminal fill.
