## Certified fixed-quantity partial reduce

`trade.rd-replay-partial-reduce-intent.v1` freezes one opposite-side market reduce-only fixed quantity, strictly smaller than executable entry quantity and increment-aligned. At its strictly later eligible open, risk/stop/target retain priority. If not preempted, Simulator fills the partial Order at phase `20`, then at phase `90` cancels both current protection Orders and submits/activates replacements at unchanged trigger prices for exactly `abs(post-fill position)`, with no intervening SourceEvent. A terminal owner before execution cancels the pending partial Order. Schedule v5 rejects a second partial or any protective-stop replacement in the same run, but permits one later final full exit. Checkpoint v17 preserves pending/filled partial Order、partial Fill、rebuilt bracket generation 与 consumed halt/resume prefix，并以冻结 Intent、最后 OrderEvent 与确定 protection identity/trigger/quantity 重验其语义；clean/resume parity is mandatory.

The certified Manifest can prove frozen halt/resume history but still does not prove a delisting settlement price. An open position reaching delisting therefore produces a non-retryable typed failure with the terminal EventKey and publishes no partial Result; the engine never invents a terminal fill.
