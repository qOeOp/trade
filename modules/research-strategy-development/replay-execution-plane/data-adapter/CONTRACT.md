# Replay Data Adapter

Owns normalized Replay input admission: manifest/ref/content-hash binding, RFC 3339 UTC and OHLC invariants, instrument lifecycle, point-in-time universe declaration, closed-bar grid/gap detection, exact funding-event ordering, executable-window selection, evidence-window slicing, and contiguous venue-risk/instrument-spec epoch schedules. Epochs are ordered half-open intervals; gaps, overlaps, unavailable snapshots, symbol/venue drift, incomplete window coverage, and schedule-hash drift are rejected. The active snapshot at timestamp `t` is resolved before any SourceEvent at `t`.

It consumes immutable data already selected by Control Plane governance. Invalid identity, future availability, pre-listing/post-delisting facts and hash drift are rejected; admissible grid gaps, incomplete instrument history and survivor-only universes become Result limitations. Instrument accounting remains one full-window invariant even when provenance snapshots change. It does not collect historical venue rules, choose datasets, repair gaps silently, generate signals, match orders, or make review decisions.
