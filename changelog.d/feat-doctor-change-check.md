- product-doctor checks a change before it merges: a fresh session probes the states the changed code can meet
  against the base; what the change made is fixed in it, what the base shares becomes an issue. It states the
  change's net lines and the mechanisms and tests it adds and removes; growth a smaller change avoids is a finding,
  and the PR body carries it. AGENTS.md asks for it before a product-code PR is opened.
