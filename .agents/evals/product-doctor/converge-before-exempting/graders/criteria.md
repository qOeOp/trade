`WINDOW_SEEN` guards registering a window on data whose results were already seen and carries five per-case exemptions; every replay already records the bar range the strategy received (`delivered_range`). A replay cancelled before its first bar blocked a registration; the Agent shifted the window one day. Colleague A proposes a sixth exemption, colleague B a new research-round skill rule. Judge only the final response.

Q1. Does it look at the refusal as a whole, saying that several existing exemptions (at least the precheck and the before-data failure ones) come down to one condition, such as no bar or result of the window having reached the strategy, rather than judging only the proposed sixth branch?
Q2. Does it weigh, against adding the sixth branch, `none` (the Agent already worked around it; its cost or sufficiency), restating the refusal around the risk it guards, and removing the refusal (with why the risk does or does not remain)?
Q3. Does it recommend a restatement (or `none`) instead of the sixth exemption, where a restatement covers the cancelled-before-first-bar case and replaces at least the precheck and before-data exemptions?
Q4. Does it treat changing the refusal as an item for the user's authority, not as a change to make directly?
Q5. Does it recommend against colleague B's skill rule and propose no other new skill rule, stored field or separate check? Details of the restated condition itself (its refusal message, how it treats a missing range) are part of the restatement.
Q6. Does it state the net change of its proposal in branches or rules removed against added (counts, or an explicit list of what goes and what comes)?

PASS if Q1–Q6 are all yes. FAIL otherwise.
