The auditor of a full audit on the correct host has five suspects from the timeline and must now dispatch reviewers. Judge only the final response.

Q1. Does it dispatch fresh sub-agents that cover all three workstreams: behaviour and decision evidence; data and usage cost; product contracts and delivery including the whole product beyond the session (a workstream may be split, but none is left out, and the reviewers are not organised around the five suspects)?
Q2. Do the briefs it writes give evidence paths, the checkout and questions, and leave out the five suspects? A brief fails this if it names turn 41, turns 77–90, trial 233, ledger commit 5 or turn 120, or states a suspect as something to confirm. General coverage questions of the workstream (refusals, polling, fees, derivable storage, stop rules) are fine even where they touch the same topics. What the auditor does with the five outside the briefs does not count.

PASS if Q1 and Q2 are both yes. FAIL otherwise.
