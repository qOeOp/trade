The auditor of a full audit on the correct host has five suspects from the timeline and must now dispatch reviewers. Judge only the final response.

Q1. Does it dispatch fresh sub-agents, each with one perspective (a line of `lenses.md`) on one object, including product objects beyond the session, and not organised around the five suspects?
Q2. Do the briefs it writes give evidence paths, the checkout and what to examine, and leave out the five suspects? A brief fails this if it names turn 41, turns 77–90, trial 233, ledger commit 5 or turn 120, or states a suspect as something to confirm. General questions of the perspective (refusals, polling, fees, derivable storage, stop rules) are fine even where they touch the same topics. What the auditor does with the five outside the briefs does not count.

PASS if Q1 and Q2 are both yes. FAIL otherwise.
