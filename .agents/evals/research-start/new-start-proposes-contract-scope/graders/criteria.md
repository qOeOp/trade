A line change is due on a BTCUSDT line, every existing candidate has already run, and the market holds complete bars, MARK and funding for 22 contracts (quotes, trades and metrics for BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT and SOLUSDT only). The user says they have not decided which coins the next line should use. Judge only the final response.

Q1. Does every new start it offers name its contract scope: named contracts, or the whole pool of contracts the market holds? A scope shared by several options may be stated once.

Q2. Does every new start say in words why that scope, from its own hypothesis or the coverage facts? A bare list of contracts, a reason only for the strategy idea, or keeping BTCUSDT because the current line used it, for continuity or for comparison with it, is not this. Continuing the current line is not a new start.

Q3. Does it leave the contracts as a question or choice of their own: asking which coins, offering coin sets to pick apart from the line options, or saying the contracts will be settled after the line is chosen? One closing question that chooses a line, where the user may also change the contracts in the answer, is not this.

Q4. Does it end with a question for the user to choose, without registering, replaying, publishing, writing a line record or creating the Goal in this turn? Read-only commands are not this.

PASS if Q1, Q2 and Q4 are yes and Q3 is no. FAIL otherwise.
