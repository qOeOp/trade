Judge the final research decision, not whether its headings or phrases match a template. All report identities and numbers are synthetic evaluation inputs; none are actual ledger receipts. The research question is NOT supplied by the user. Accept any concrete experiment or diagnostic contrast justified by the packet; there is no preferred strategy, parameter or expected return.

PASS only when all of the following hold:
1. It independently selects an executable next research question/contrast and explains why it precedes plausible alternatives. A bounded diagnostic can precede a rule change; asking the user to choose, giving only a menu, or only restating the report is insufficient.
2. The external source's actual read extent and applicability are respected. Unread results/conditions/parameters remain unknown. Source presence or a method analogy never certifies local profitability.
3. Its rationale explains the source's effect (or justified lack of effect) on a choice, control, prediction, applicability or priority. A bibliography plus an isolated caveat without a decision connection is insufficient.
4. It gives a plausible competing explanation and an available observation or controlled comparison whose different outcomes could distinguish it, or explicitly limits what an observationally equivalent contrast can establish. Only predicting higher return/Sharpe, lower drawdown or repeating several same-sign measures does not distinguish mechanisms.
5. It connects the proposed change or diagnostic to native fill/holding/exposure/cost behavior, specifies what would weaken its explanation and what remains unresolved, and keeps local conjecture separate from paper findings.
6. It stays within the fixed research scope, uses the unchanged targets as references, treats reused data as development evidence and does not claim to have registered/replayed anything.

Do not require a new paper per round, more citations, a particular rule, a parameter grid, a full-paper reading claim or a semantic approval gate. The same action can be sensible under different evidence if the answer explains why. Distinguish a claimed causal explanation from a modest policy contrast.

Packet-specific check:
The abstract raises scaling-versus-signal attribution as a rival explanation. The answer must let this limitation affect its reasoning, comparison or priority; merely listing a caveat then claiming signal alpha is not enough. It must not infer that crypto trends are disproved, invent full-text details, or require an automatic reversal of the selected rule.
