# Perspectives

A full audit exercises each line, recorded with `doctor.py run DIR ITEM SUBJECT`; brackets name the
subjects (`any`: one object of the audit's choosing; `rotating`: at least the one longest unexamined in earlier records).

- **value** [any]: what nobody uses, what the user asked for and never got; from use counts and their words.
- **results** [any]: one number against an outside original, a conservation sum, or an answer built in.
- **flow** [single pool]: research end to end on a temporary ledger and market, `line select` to
  `line close`, then Paper and `paper compare`, then Live's research-side refusals, short windows,
  no credential and no run.
- **parity** [any]: one market history through replay, Paper and Live's adapter fakes.
- **research** [any]: noise, a true edge and a leaking strategy through the tests and verdicts; each
  line's history read through the product's read commands, never ad hoc SQL.
- **external** [rotating]: what outside parties say now against what the product keeps or assumes,
  including terms and licences (legal questions go to the user).
- **consumers** [ledger report cli ide]: one state through every reader; each stored column's reader.
- **storage** [rotating]: backup restore and ledger rebuild on copies; growth per unit of use.
- **gates** [empty failed partial]: every admission, eligibility and refusal with a state never
  started, failed or cut short; a stated reason is tested, not trusted, and a good case must pass.
- **faults** [live docker]: fault fixtures (live loopback, fake Docker).
- **interrupt** [any]: a many-step operation stopped after each step and resumed, on fakes that lose
  what the stopped step took.
- **operation** [rotating]: what an operator learns, and when, as a service degrades or a disk fills.
- **cost** [replay import reports ledger]: each long path at three or more sizes (`doctor.py cost`).
- **structure** [any]: layers, cycles, native capability rebuilt, one change that touches many places.
- **size** [any]: net lines by area and type of change since the last audit, attributed to feature,
  guard, patch on patch, residue or bypass; counts alone show nothing. A removal claim is a removal run.
- **checks** [rotating]: whether a test, eval or CI gate fails for a wrong product and passes a right one.
- **delivery** [rotating]: what CI, release and merge cost and where they waited, failed or were
  repeated (GitHub's own records).
- **lifecycle** [rotating]: first install, upgrade, idle, moving host, on copies.
- **pages** [empty records offline reload]: each IDE page in a real browser on its own
  `ide/ide.py serve`, never the user's; the artifact is the drawn text. An unknown address can draw
  the first page; judge at realistic density.
- **usability** [rotating]: keyboard, contrast, narrow screens, and a reader without context deciding
  from what is shown.
- **words** [any]: a document or help followed literally; published pages against the code they name.
- **security** [rotating]: what each process may reach, dependencies; no credential is read.
- **agents** [any]: from transcripts, skills that did not fire, reports that claim more than was done,
  duplicated work.
- **burden** [rotating]: what the user was asked, made to decode or wait for.
- **governance** [rotating]: rules nothing holds, copies that disagree, rules whose reason has gone.
- **fixed** [any]: `doctor.py recheck` on closed issues and the rows the index header links.
- **self** [rotating]: what the user found first, rerun claims overturned, lenses that ran and found
  nothing for several audits (say so).
