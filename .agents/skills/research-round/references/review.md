# Recommendation and review

Read when writing a recommendation or resolving REVIEW_DUE. The stop counts research trials since
the latest recommendation's based-on commit (or line creation); an early recommendation moves
that reference. The recommendation clears the stop. This is review cadence, not independent
validation or a limit on a research direction.

Recommendation selects versions; priority selects experiments (feedback.md). Write a
recommendation at REVIEW_DUE or when the recommended version changes; a per-run interpretation and
priority do not require one. Review changed conclusions, competing explanations and the information
another experiment can add.

Use research status at the cited commit for counts and its full comparison table, including older
versions and other lines. Never reconstruct counts or progress from memory. Each row carries its
execution identity (runtime contract, execution environment, image); the table's
`execution_identity` is the line's, that of its latest research trial since `line select`. Comparable candidates are
those with economic evidence on the line's identity, not merely passed audits; runs of another
identity are history, never ranked against it. A recommendation's subjects name every candidate in
`candidates`, the table's candidates of the line's identity. Its body weighs the recommended version and each candidate whose evidence
or availability changed since the previous recommendation (every candidate at the first) on return, drawdown, Sharpe, win rate,
costs, exposure, profit mechanism and uncertainty using replay report, and cites the previous
recommendation for the rest instead of restating it. State trade-offs and retained references; unavailable measures
keep their reasons, and invalid or diagnostic-only runs stay separate and are not recommended.

The table's non_dominated set describes economically available runs on the same window, universe
and execution identity, using annual return, drawdown, Sharpe and win rate. It is neither eligibility nor a
ranking or gate.
