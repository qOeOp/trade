# Feedback and next experiment

Interpretation, search_exposure and priority are judgments (`judgment add`, `research state`; their
`--help` gives binding, correction and history).

## Interpret evidence

Use product comparisons with qualified, comparable controls. Resolve comparability refusals by
choosing a valid control or replaying under a common identity; never construct a substitute paired
series. A result whose intervals all cross zero is uncertain; development evidence remains
conditional.

Before the next registration, interpret the previous completed result with its applicability and
reassessment conditions. Invalid runs retried through `retry_of` receive repair evidence instead
(diagnosis.md). Losing, insignificant or zero-activity results do not exclude a direction. Record
self-computed views as search_exposure, naming each variant compared: analyses of replayed runs,
skill-required ones included, and views of signals, dates or counts; product trial counts come from
the ledger.

## Choose the next experiment

Rank rule changes, sizing and allocation included, by the gap to the user's targets, expected
effect, uncertainty, mechanism support, previous exploration, information value and execution cost.
A sizing or allocation change tests its account effect against the targets, not competing
explanations. A run that only scales position size or a notional limit on the same path neither
counts toward nor clears a due change of approach: its paired difference is nearly a fixed multiple of the
control's returns, so its intervals say nothing about scale, and you never report them as if they did. Write each action's standing on these grounds in a priority judgment citing its runs
and ledger commit, separately from the candidate recommendation. Negative evidence updates priority
and reassessment conditions, not permission to research a direction.

The leading action names its supporting report slice or section, measure and direction, affected
duties and the policy explanation it tests. Separate observed facts from conjecture. Parameter
choices need supporting evidence or an explicit statement that they are exploratory. Information
value is the ability to distinguish competing explanations. Aim each change at an effect its
comparison can detect: at or above the comparison's detectable difference (in `minimum_effect`
units; one-sided alpha, so about 70% power against the two-sided intervals, and it scales with
position size), or a grid whose points spread the change far enough to reach it. Moving one
constant inside a range whose neighbours already read alike tests nothing new. Repeated metrics,
labels and registrations on the same development data are not independent evidence.

## Change approach

When a change of approach is due (SKILL.md), first state in one line how many research runs the
line has made on its development window (`research status`), how many changes of approach it has
recorded, whether the recommended version changed since the last one, and how far its annual
return moved over the counted runs, beside their detectable annual difference; it is a reading for
the user, not an option to close the line. Then weigh two or three approaches inside this line,
never one an earlier `Change of approach.` judgment chose unless you name it and the new evidence. Each
changes a part of the strategy the counted runs left alone, aims above their detectable annual
difference (`hypothesis compare`'s `detectable_annual_return_difference_80pct_normal_approx`;
undistinguished does not mean no better) and keeps the control's contracts and inputs
(hypothesis.md); say in one line that an approach needing more belongs to another line, never as
an option. Draw on hypotheses other lines tested (as ideas for this line's source), recombined
parts of different mechanisms and sources outside the ledger (`research-sources`,
`video-evidence`), not only mechanism keywords. A fusion merges a closed line's candidate rule,
chosen where its `research history` holds up in years this line's candidate fails, into this
line's source as one change; `rationale.local` names each part and those years, which never count
toward its targets. When the bottleneck is unclear (opportunities,
signal quality, costs or capital use), the first step tells the leading explanations apart. Each
approach names its first experiment and control, the outcome that would change the next step,
and what its source states apart from your own design.
Choose the one you rank first and, before its first registration, record the change as a priority
judgment whose body begins `Change of approach.`, ranks the approaches with the reason for the
choice, says who chose (`chosen by the Agent` or `chosen by the user`), names each scale-only run
left out with its reason and each counted run as a subject (`--subject trial=N`): that restarts the
count (SKILL.md). An idea needing more contracts or inputs is a reading there, never an exit. Tell the user once, in that round's message and asking nothing: the reading above, the
recommended version's readings research-start's recommendation.md lists, the approaches, the one
chosen and why, and the judgment. Then carry out its first experiment; an approach the user names
later replaces yours.
