# Diagnosing a run

Read a completed run through replay report: eligibility first, then execution validity, causal
clocks and readiness, exposure beside drawdown, and economic attribution (attribution.md).

## Validity before performance

Product eligibility and masking determine which economic measures are available. Never recover
masked values elsewhere. Explain absent activity or reconciliation failures from signal/readiness,
opportunity rules and native order/event evidence. Such runs are diagnostic only, not economic
losses or reasons to change priority.

Failed prechecks and invalid, failed or unfinished executions receive repair evidence identifying
the original and the remedy, not performance interpretation or negative direction evidence; their
trials still count. Publish repaired source, precheck and review again, then register anew with
retry_of only if a registration was consumed. Root repair before its first report follows
template.md. Product defects must be fixed and merged to main before retrying.

## Causal comparability

Check each plan's decision, submit and fill timestamps after precheck and replay: fills must be
strictly later than the decision bar's close. A violation invalidates the economic interpretation
even if the audit passed; withdraw it and repair through the publication/review/registration path.
Register initialization conditions for changed timeframes or indicator lengths, and report when
each side could first decide. Readiness differences are not evidence of the proposed mechanism.

## Economic reading

Read capital use beside drawdown (nautilus-report-analysis capital.md) and the profit bridge beside
account return. Size is part of the policy and its account effect counts against the user's
unchanged targets. Quality measures remain descriptive and need uncertainty and exposure context;
less exposure alone does not establish better quality. Follow report definition notes for units,
populations and the meaning of decompositions; attribution.md governs policy explanations.
