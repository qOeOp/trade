# Hypothesis and preregistration

Read when choosing a parent and control, checking sources or preparing a registration body.

## Support and scope

Read retained source evidence and corrections before searching its gaps (`research-sources`; video
material: `video-evidence`). Retain originals, excerpts and query logs through `evidence add` before
citing them in a registration. Distinguish external evidence, local diagnosis and conjecture in
rationale.external/local/conjecture. rationale.external holds citations (DOI, arXiv id or URL)
bearing on this change, else the named `research-sources` queries, indexes and result, absence
worded as that skill reports it. `sources` names the bundles holding those originals or that search
log. Absence goes in external, never in local. External support identifies its location and market,
window, frequency, costs and validation conditions; unread conditions stay unknown. State whether it
supports the mechanism, the parameter or only the method. Explain how the source shapes the
question, control, prediction or priority; if it is only background, explain why the local question
still comes first without inventing an effect on the decision. Local evidence names run and commit.

Your memory of the development window's prices, funding and events is look-ahead no replay check
catches: it is neither external evidence nor local diagnosis and supports no rationale. For each
event the rationale relies on, state whether it was knowable before the decision.

Describe the economic mechanism, who bears its cost and why it could persist under this line's
conditions. Name a proxy and its limits where inputs do not observe the proposed mechanism.

Choose the parent for the change and the control for the comparison, with separate reasons.
Any published lineage candidate can be a parent regardless of recommendation or past performance;
no approval or eligibility challenge follows from that choice.

## Change and prediction

Register one concrete rule change: publish every grid candidate with it as one `--difference` text,
which the registration stores as its change. Several affected duties can belong to one coupled
policy. Derived quantities and inactive-method parameters do not create additional changes. Use
candidate headers to locate relevant policies, defaults and applicability; connect those choices to
the diagnosis and register affected ids in changed_duties. Use duty-directory.md when their scope
is unclear.

For each changed duty, predict the direction of a catalogue measure and the native fill or holding
behavior expected to produce it, where the control's report shows the change can act: the days,
plans or drawdown window that determine that reading. Shared measures are shared observations. Predictions must be
refutable and distinguish the proposed explanation from alternatives; profit improvement alone
does not identify a mechanism.

Before execution, specify the response to improved, not_significant, loss, zero_activity and
invalid in prediction.outcomes. Conditions and outcomes are frozen with the registration; later
interpretation belongs in judgments.

## Body and execution identity

Use the root or derived JSON template linked by SKILL.md (`registration add --help` for what the
lock binds). A root has no parent or control and empty changed_duties; a derivation names its
parent, control and a prediction for every changed duty. Comparison design is declared before
results and applies only to the registered control; a root has no paired comparison.
