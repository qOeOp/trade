# Backtest and qualification scenario

This scenario evaluates a frozen candidate independently. Qualification keeps the write-once Intake Receipt in
immutable history and projects the current branch: `EVALUATING`, terminal attempt disposition, or Eligibility.

## Entry

Product Edge requests an independent review of an existing R&D-owned Candidate. Research has already
frozen TrialFamily, its immutable exhaustive Census Frontier, Artifact, PIT rule, purge or embargo, costs,
capacity assumptions, budget, falsifier, complete cross-family semantic predecessor frontier, origin feedback
frontier, and any independence basis committed before protected feedback. Research has also committed a terminal
`SELECTED_FOR_QUALIFICATION` Research Selection Disposition cross-bound to that exact falsifier, stop rule,
exploratory frontier, Candidate, and Census Frontier. The review request binds its stable
identity and canonical typed meaning plus exactly one `READY_FOR_SELECTION` Iteration Decision with the same
decision-policy version and evidence cut. Candidate and Intake bind one exact preregistered protected decision-policy
identity and version plus one frozen Protected Robustness Plan. The plan fixes required time-window, regime,
instrument-slice, perturbation, and reasonable parameter-neighborhood cells; coverage, metric, tolerance,
threshold, aggregation, missing-cell, and stop rules. An axis may contain multiple cells, and the exact complete
plan-cell-set digest is fixed before replay. Qualification verifies that pack and alone reserves and consumes holdout.
After `ADMITTED`, Qualification freezes the exact Strategy Artifact, requested PIT scope, exact PIT Market
Snapshot identity, Universe Selection Record identity and digest, snapshot/correction rule, replay-configuration digest, Runtime kernel, simulator, cost, slippage, and capacity model versions, and that exact protected policy pair in
one Protected Replay Request.

## Value path

Qualification sends the frozen candidate to an isolated protected replay. Market Data supplies the exact
point-in-time replay inputs. Native Replay uses the deterministic trading kernel, Sim Exchange creates
simulated effects, and Run Result records actual consumed inputs and outcomes. Protected Evaluation applies
the preregistered decision rules only after every consumed execution identity exactly equals its request field.
Once the protected request exists, even Backtest admission rejection commits
a request-bound `RUN_REJECTED` result. A rejected or invalid terminal replay closes as Protected Attempt Disposition.
Only an exact `TERMINAL_RESULT` whose consumed identities equal the Protected Replay Request may be assessed under
the bound protected decision-policy version and close as an initial or renewed Eligibility Fact; only an
in-progress or unknown result projects `EVALUATING` from the unchanged `ADMITTED` receipt and protected request.
Before economics or robustness assessment, Qualification consumes the sealed complete finite non-empty
`diagnosticCategorySet` and its content digest. Every independently supported member and per-category decisive
evidence remain preserved. Qualification first validates one finite, non-empty, duplicate-free subset of the
supported categories. Empty, duplicate, unknown, `NO_EXECUTION_DEFECT`-mixed, or `UNRESOLVED_FAILURE`-mixed sets
close `DIAGNOSTIC_UNRESOLVED` before category precedence. Only for a structurally valid set does any
execution-defect member preempt economics and close `DIAGNOSTIC_INVALID`;
`UNRESOLVED_FAILURE` is valid only as a singleton unresolved set; `NO_EXECUTION_DEFECT` is valid only as a
singleton and is the only set that may reach a passing assessment. A decisive
Backtest runner-readiness, backpressure, resource-exhaustion, or service-outage result is
`BACKTEST_OPERATIONAL`, not `RUNTIME_KERNEL` or `SIMULATOR`; it closes as `DIAGNOSTIC_INVALID`, applies the
preregistered holdout disposition, and creates no Eligibility Fact. The operational profile, run attempt,
evidence, and protected detail remain Qualification-only and never return to R&D, Product Edge, or Governance.
Observability may expose only the protected run's bounded public terminal outcome `CLOSED_NOT_QUALIFIED` or
`QUALIFIED`, a type-opaque non-dereferenceable reference, and source-frontier freshness. Protected phase,
latency, terminal timing, and timing-derived fields are forbidden. It never exposes a generic terminal
disposition, internal reason, or internal state. Every
`REPLAY_REJECTED`, `REPLAY_INVALID`, `DIAGNOSTIC_INVALID`, `DIAGNOSTIC_UNRESOLVED`, `ASSESSMENT_INVALID`, and
`INELIGIBLE` terminal is byte-equivalently normalized to `CLOSED_NOT_QUALIFIED`; positive `QUALIFIED` remains
exact. It must not expose, group, filter, label, count, alert, or derive any aggregate from the protected
diagnostic set membership; exploratory categories remain observable under their separate disclosure policy.
Qualification repeats the exact plan-cell-set digest and enumerates every plan-required cell exactly once as `PASS`, `FAIL`,
`NOT_APPLICABLE_ACCEPTED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE_REJECTED`, or `MISSING`. A complete pass requires at least one
applicable cell, all applicable cells passing, and every non-applicable cell accepted under the frozen adjudication
policy. Missing, duplicate, unknown, mismatched, or all-not-applicable assessments are `INCOMPLETE_INVALID` and
commit `ASSESSMENT_INVALID` with holdout closure and no Eligibility Fact; `COMPLETE_FAIL` produces `INELIGIBLE`,
while only `COMPLETE_PASS` produces `QUALIFIED`.
Qualification returns the write-once Intake Receipt directly to Product Edge as the authoritative review-request
outcome; its separate status summary never substitutes for that receipt. A `QUALIFIED` Eligibility Fact binds the
economic-condition and evaluated cost/capacity-model versions plus the capacity ceiling Governance and Risk must enforce.

## Owner handoffs

Product Edge → Qualification is the stable request-correlated review, including origin-to-current bounded
protected-feedback frontiers. R&D → Qualification is the frozen Candidate, exact Research Selection
Disposition, falsifier, exploratory frontier, and cross-family ancestry handoff.
Qualification → Backtest requests protected replay. Market Data → Backtest supplies frozen facts.
Backtest → Qualification returns canonical evidence. Qualification → Strategy Governance publishes an
Eligibility State fact, including revocation, only when one exists. Qualification separately returns the committed Intake Receipt
to Product Edge; the bounded status view remains a read model. Protected Attempt Disposition remains Qualification-owned
and is exposed only as a bounded categorical Product status.

## Proof

Proof always includes the frozen Candidate, its exact `SELECTED_FOR_QUALIFICATION` Research Selection
Disposition and Intent falsifier, TrialFamily, immutable exhaustive Census Frontier, complete
cross-family predecessor frontier, protected-feedback and protected-attempt frontiers, cumulative holdout
disposition, preregistration, stable review request, and its write-once correlated Intake Receipt.
`NOT_ADMITTED` ends before protected replay with no holdout consumption. After `ADMITTED`,
`IN_PROGRESS_OR_UNKNOWN` adds the Protected Replay Request and reserved holdout but no terminal fact;
`RUN_REJECTED` or `INVALID_REPLAY_EVIDENCE` adds the sealed Protected Run Result, holdout closure, and exactly one
`REPLAY_REJECTED` or `REPLAY_INVALID` Attempt Disposition with no Eligibility Fact. A valid `TERMINAL_RESULT`
with an execution-defect diagnostic, including `BACKTEST_OPERATIONAL`, adds `DIAGNOSTIC_INVALID`;
`UNRESOLVED_FAILURE` adds
`DIAGNOSTIC_UNRESOLVED`; both close holdout with no assessment or Eligibility Fact. `VALID_ECONOMIC_FAILURE`
can produce only `INELIGIBLE`. A `TERMINAL_RESULT` with `INCOMPLETE_INVALID` instead adds `ASSESSMENT_INVALID`
and holdout closure with no Eligibility Fact. A valid
complete assessment adds the sealed Protected Run Result and exactly one `INELIGIBLE` or `QUALIFIED` Eligibility Fact cross-bound to
the exact Protected Replay Request, exact result, protected decision-policy identity and version, and verified request/result
equality. It also binds its interval and, when qualified, its enforceable economic-condition version,
cost/capacity-model version, and capacity ceiling; later
expiry or revocation is an immutable successor Eligibility Fact. `UNAVAILABLE` binds only the requested
Candidate and summary phase identity and fabricates no current authoritative phase fact.

The Eligibility Fact also repeats the exact protected decision-policy identity and version from Candidate Intake
and the Protected Replay Request.

## Development outcome

- **Beneficiary** - independent evaluators, governance operators, and capital owners deciding whether a frozen candidate is deployable.
- **Observable outcome** - one request closes as intake rejection, protected attempt disposition, or eligibility with exact holdout, cost, capacity, and consumed-input lineage.
- **Harm if unchanged** - overfit candidates, mismatched simulators, hidden trial families, or reused holdout could be promoted as economically valid evidence.
- **Terminal negative** - non-selection or incomplete intake is `NOT_ADMITTED`; rejected or invalid replay has no Eligibility Fact; valid failure is `INELIGIBLE`, and none may reach Governance as qualified.

## Fail closed and forbidden transitions

- Incomplete preregistration, a missing mutable non-exhaustive or late-divergent Census Frontier, or unavailable PIT data blocks protected evaluation.
- Unresolved ancestry, a late independence basis, stale feedback frontier, or a new request identity for the same intake is `NOT_ADMITTED` and creates no holdout attempt.
- A terminal Research stop creates no Candidate and never reaches intake. A missing selected-only disposition, or
  one mismatched to Candidate, falsifier, stop rule, exploratory frontier, or Census, closes as `NOT_ADMITTED`
  before protected replay and consumes no holdout.
- A protected request cannot be rejected in place; Backtest rejection must produce a request-bound `RUN_REJECTED` result so Qualification can close holdout custody.
- Any missing, substituted, or mismatched Artifact, PIT scope, PIT Market Snapshot identity, Universe Selection Record identity or digest, snapshot rule, replay configuration, Runtime kernel, simulator, cost, slippage, or capacity model produces `INVALID_REPLAY_EVIDENCE`, closes the attempt under preregistered holdout treatment, and produces no Eligibility Fact.
- Rejected, invalid, nonterminal, or request/result-mismatched evidence cannot create or renew Eligibility; changing
  the protected decision-policy version requires a successor evaluation and cannot reinterpret an earlier result.
- Candidate, `ADMITTED` Intake, Protected Replay Request, and Eligibility must carry the same protected
  decision-policy identity and version; missing or substituted policy identity/version fails closed.
- Backtest cannot grant eligibility, and Qualification cannot activate a strategy.
- Protected results cannot return to R&D for tuning in the same trial family.
- Protected diagnostic categories and category-derived aggregates cannot enter Dashboard, metrics, alerts,
  health scores, or research-funnel projections; bounded operational status must remain category-indistinguishable.
- Passing one protected test does not erase multiple-testing, cost, capacity, or holdout-consumption limits.
- Qualification cannot emit `QUALIFIED` from one attractive aggregate. Candidate Intake must first admit the plan
  under the exact Qualification-owned robustness-adequacy policy, including at least two non-overlapping windows,
  at least two materially distinct regimes with one adverse regime, and accepted axis-specific coverage or
  non-applicability bases. The complete Protected Robustness Plan must
  meet its preregistered cell coverage, tolerance, threshold, aggregation, and missing-cell rules; omitted or
  post-result-selected cells fail closed.
- Every plan-required cell resolves exactly once and an axis may contain multiple cells. Non-applicability needs
  an explicit pre-result basis accepted by the bound policy; missing, stale, rejected, or policy-mismatched basis
  is rejected, and an all-not-applicable census is `INCOMPLETE_INVALID` with `ASSESSMENT_INVALID`, not a pass or
  Eligibility Fact.
- Purge and embargo derivation, family-aware multiplicity, and attempt-frontier basis are versioned and frozen
  through Candidate, Intake, Protected Replay Request, Run Result, and Eligibility. A changed basis creates a
  successor evaluation and never reinterprets a consumed holdout.
- Governance or Risk cannot widen the capacity ceiling or bind it to another Candidate, condition, model, or Eligibility Fact version.
- Changing TrialFamily, Candidate, Artifact, shell, or request identity cannot reset cumulative consumption from rejected, invalid, unknown, or terminal protected attempts.
