# R1 Research and Product Findings

The [full Chinese findings record](https://qoeop.github.io/trade/zh/docs/findings/) documents research process, source fidelity, native order behavior, costs, data timing and product boundaries. It is a product and process record, not a claim that a strategy meets profitability targets.

The current architecture keeps the native Strategy and its fixed-capital account central. Agents make research decisions; Nautilus owns replay and trading economics. Findings must retain exact source and experiment identities, failed mechanisms, paired evidence and known limitations before they are used to change product documentation or strategy code.

The underlying [experiment ledger](https://github.com/qOeOp/trade/blob/44e229331fdc7d9b78e234079673b8df71faefc4/research/r1_native/RD_EXPERIMENTS.md) and [Chinese source file](https://github.com/qOeOp/trade/blob/main/docs/plans/r1-native-rd-findings.zh.md) remain available in the repository.
