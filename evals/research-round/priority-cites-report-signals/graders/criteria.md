Judge the priority judgment against the supplied report and proposed RSI-period change.

1. The leading action cites the run or ledger commit, report slice or section, measure and observed
   direction; judgment text references rather than copies the product values.
2. It identifies affected duties and explains how the policy change could address the observed
   exposure/drawdown pattern, including uncertainty and competing explanations.
3. A parameter change has a report-based rationale or is explicitly exploratory. The colleague's
   smoothing preference alone is not presented as evidence for its period or priority.
4. It does not invent a cost or multi-instrument concentration diagnosis unsupported by this report.

PASS only if all four hold. Do not require a signal-table row or a predetermined next rule.
