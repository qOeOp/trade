# Price-action source map for R1 research

This is a source inventory and hypothesis map, not a claim that Al Brooks's E-mini/forex lessons have a proven Binance-perpetual edge or that Ronnie follows Brooks's rules. Ronnie's reviewed source charts use candle structure, support/resistance, trend lines and retracement tiers; his exact discretionary information set is not recoverable from videos alone. The current Strategy is a bounded researcher proxy, not a full replica. Read the official materials below before selecting any new rule.

## Primary sources and transfer boundary

- Al Brooks, [How to Trade Price Action manual](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/how-to-trade-price-action-manual/): context and market cycle precede candle patterns; first distinguish trend from range, then breakout from channel, then choose stops and targets. The published table of contents covers bar counting, breakouts, channels, support/resistance, reversals, trade management and scaling.
- Al Brooks, [2018 CME Advanced Gap Techniques, 19 slides](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/2018-July-20-CME-Advanced-Gap-Techniques.pdf), linked from [the author's event page](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/webinars-lectures/tradersexpo-back-to-the-futures-at-cme-chicago/). A gap may be a nonoverlap between the bars on either side of a breakout bar, a body-only separation, or the space between a breakout point and a pullback that fails to return there. He also discusses open-to-previous-close gaps, which must not be assumed to have the same meaning in a continuous 24/7 perpetual market. The slides warn that most gaps have no useful follow-through; the *context and persistence* are the testable mechanism. The original event concerned E-mini scalping, so time horizon, venue and fee transfer are hypotheses.
- Al Brooks, [2016 strong leg versus trading range presentation](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Al-Brooks-Webinar-Distinguishing-Strong-Legs-TR-vs-Trends-May-3-2016.pdf), especially slides 36–38: a successful range breakout has a strong close away from the old range and follow-through over the next bar or two; apparent strong legs within a range can be traps. This is not a universal numerical threshold for crypto.
- [Brooks Trading Course price-action glossary](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/price-action-trading-terms-glossary/) defines breakout, follow-through, measuring/exhaustion/micro/body gaps, high/low 2, and swing versus scalp. [The official course index](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/trade-forex-price-action/) organizes lessons on market cycle (12), gaps (11), breakouts (15), trading ranges (18), support/resistance (19), measured moves (20), reversals (21–22), management/stops (30–35). The index identifies topics, not verified performance.
- In [Brooks's own breakout Q&A](https://www.brookstradingcourse.com/ask-al/breakouts/), a swing can tolerate a pullback while its breakout premise remains valid. This is a useful counterexample to generic early-exit rules; the exact breakout low must be defined from pre-existing bars.
