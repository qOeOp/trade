# Ronnie R-1 experiment ledger

Research goal: `01a11518-fb33-7853-a31c-b8c43df5be60`. The exit targets are a reproducible, fee- and funding-inclusive **single-account** annualized return above 20%, a closed-trade win rate near 60%, and then the highest defensible Sharpe ratio, with drawdown reported. No exchange trading is authorized.

Operational comparison convention: annualize the native final-equity ratio over actual UTC calendar days using 365 days/year; one closed native position is one trade even with partial exits; a win has positive native position `realized_pnl`, which includes fill commissions in settlement currency. Funding is charged to the native account and reflected in equity, but is not allocated to this position-level win flag. For mechanism screening, interpret "near 60%" as 55-65% inclusive, declared before selecting any new strategy variant. Report the native daily-return Sharpe using both 252 and 365 annualization; prefer 365 for this continuous crypto market. This convention does not turn development data into independent evidence.

Product and process findings for later blueprint reconciliation are in `docs/plans/r1-native-rd-findings.zh.md`.

This file records source observations, diagnostic hypotheses, exact data/result locators, outcomes, and next decisions. The 2025-10-07 to 2026-10-07 Binance futures sample and the 2026-09 to 2026-10 sample have already been inspected. Further results from them are development evidence, even if the code was registered before a particular rerun. All attempted variants and reads remain visible here; overlapping slices are not independent holdouts. Use predeclared mechanism changes, paired native replay, multiple-testing-aware uncertainty and later forward evidence to address this exposure. Lack of a fresh historical holdout does not stop the research.

## Diagnostic D05: how many stopped trades first reached +1R?

**Question and read:** Before investing in a native partial-exit variant to raise the win rate, measure the opportunity in the clean D04 account without simulating new orders. `analyze_exits.py` joins each native closed position's entry fill to its linked native stop trigger, then scans the reused LAST five-minute catalog between native entry and exit timestamps. The strict measure excludes both boundary bars; the permissive measure includes both, even though its high/low may have occurred before entry or after exit. The code and `results/2026-10-07-exit-attribution-d05.json` reproduce this diagnostic; the JSON SHA-256 is `3291fc8f71209cc98a974011a8bb8dffb19d1481dbdf6478c7cfbdd08725d0d6`. The native positions and orders SHA-256 values are `31b67b2253a4f0515b0ec991be9db621fbddffbd424bb6299ba9db358462c246` and `6cbb2150adf8bea91292f6c336887049e5e2aa74a3887e7b3d655eb9922eb209`.

**Observed path:** 1,625 closed native positions include 595 positive, 1,027 closed by stop-market fills, 590 by limit target fills and eight by market fills. Of the stopped positions, 243 had a full intervening five-minute bar reach +1R relative to their actual entry fill and planned stop; including both ambiguous boundary bars raises that count to 257. At +1.5R the counts are 90 and 98. Thirty positions have no full intervening bar. These are price-path bounds, not alternate fills or net returns. The six stopped positions whose permissive boundary bar reaches +2R show why bar event order matters.

**Decision:** If *every* one of the 257 permissively observed +1R stopped positions became a positive trade and the trade set stayed fixed, the win rate would be at most `(595 + 257) / 1625 = 52.43%`, below the predeclared 55-65% interpretation of "near 60%". A +1R partial exit/breakeven rule alone therefore cannot close this Goal on the D04 trade set; costs and changed slot occupancy can only be evaluated by a new native run. R-1s first takes profit at 2R, so its staged exit likewise does not directly convert the 1,027 pre-target stop paths. The frozen 2018-22 X-2 study reported 61% for P1BE but lower total R; it used a different period and an hourly approximation, and does not overturn this current native-path diagnostic. Continue with source-chart-checked entry/context selection and separate native R-1s implementation acceptance, retaining D05 as a mechanism screen rather than an economic candidate.
